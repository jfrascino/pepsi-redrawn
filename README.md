# Pepsi, Redrawn

Every primary Pepsi logo from 1898 to today, in an interactive WebGL piece: each mark drains like a glass, is redrawn in light as the next one, and fills with fizz. Stop on any year to read who designed it, how it was made and why it changed, or take it apart into its inks.

**Open it:** https://jfrascino.github.io/pepsi-redrawn/

- 22 primary marks plus a "Today" stop, using vector reproductions from Logopedia and Wikimedia Commons, checked against U.S. trademark records and the Library of Congress registration sheets.
- Every dossier lists its sources; designer credits are marked Documented, Attributed or Unconfirmed.

An independent design history, not affiliated with or endorsed by PepsiCo. Pepsi, Pepsi-Cola and the Pepsi Globe are trademarks of PepsiCo, Inc., shown here for historical commentary.
